Questions

Good to know before you call

Short answers to the questions that come up first. Anything else — we're one message away.

Ask on WhatsApp
How is pricing decided?
Pricing is quoted after a free consultation, based on guest count, city and functions. Full details and inclusions for each package are shared on request.
How does choosing vendors work?
For every category — photographers, caterers, videographers and decorators — you get three vetted options at your package's price point. You choose; no take-it-or-leave-it names.
What does Baraati handle once I've chosen?
Everything else. Baraati manages the schedule, the vendors and day-of logistics, with one team to call instead of five vendors who've never spoken to each other.
Is social content included?
Yes — reels and social content are part of every package, from a 30-second highlight to a full multi-function library.
Which package covers the whole wedding?
The Grand Baraati covers Haldi to reception, managed end-to-end, with a wedding manager on-site. Prefer to pick functions individually? The Custom Baraati lets you build it function by function.
Where do you plan weddings?
Baraati plans and coordinates weddings across Delhi NCR.
